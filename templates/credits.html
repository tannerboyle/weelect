About & credits

weelect helps you find the officials who represent you. It is an independent, nonpartisan project and is not affiliated with any government agency, political party or campaign. Information may be out of date; confirm with official sources.

★

Data

  • Address matching and district boundaries: U.S. Census Bureau Geocoder.
  • Federal officials: unitedstates/congress-legislators (CC0 public domain). Photos: unitedstates/images.
  • State legislators and some mayors: Open States people data (CC0).
  • House votes: Office of the Clerk, U.S. House of Representatives. Senate votes: Voteview (UCLA), linking to Senate.gov records.
★

Maps

State and territory outlines and the national background outline are derived from the U.S. Census Bureau, 2023 Cartographic Boundary Files, cb_2023_us_state_500k (census.gov), simplified and re-projected (Albers equal-area). U.S. Government works are not subject to copyright; the Census Bureau asks that it be cited as the source. These generalized boundaries are for illustration only and are not legal boundaries.

Fonts

State & territory flags

Flag images are from Wikimedia Commons. Each file's Commons page lists its license; all are marked public domain except Mississippi, whose flag design the State of Mississippi states "can be freely used by members of the public." No attribution is required by these licenses; sources are listed for transparency. Flags may carry the Commons "insignia" notice (government symbols that may have usage restrictions unrelated to copyright).
