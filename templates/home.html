Who represents you?

Enter a home address to see the federal and state officials elected to speak for you — from Washington to your statehouse.

Your address is sent to the U.S. Census geocoder to find your districts and, where available, to Google’s Civic Information service to find polling places. We Elect does not save it in a database or log it, and it is never placed in the web address.

We Elect exists to renew a sense of civic responsibility and a healthy public square: know who speaks for you, read the strongest case on every side, and decide for yourself. Independent and nonpartisan.
